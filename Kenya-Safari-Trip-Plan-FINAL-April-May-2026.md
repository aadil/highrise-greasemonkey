## MAY 8 - SIGHTSEEING OPTIONS

With a full day in Nairobi, here are recommended activities:

### Option A: Lake Naivasha Day Trip
- **Drive:** ~1.5-2 hours from Nairobi
- **Activities:**
  - Boat ride on the lake (hippos, birds)
  - Crescent Island walking safari (giraffes, zebras, wildebeest)
  - Hell's Gate National Park (cycling, gorge walking)
- **Best for:** Active families who want one more wildlife experience

### Option B: Nairobi City Experiences
- **Morning:** David Sheldrick Elephant Orphanage (11am-12pm, book in advance)
- **Midday:** Giraffe Centre (hand-feed giraffes)
- **Afternoon:** Karen Blixen Museum or shopping at Maasai Market
- **Best for:** Relaxed pace, great for kids

### Option C: Nairobi National Park
- **Morning game drive:** Only national park bordering a capital city
- **Unique:** See lions, rhinos with Nairobi skyline in background
- **Duration:** Half day
- **Best for:** One more safari experience close to city
